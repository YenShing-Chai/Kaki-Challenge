Direction B · Playful Buddy

Warm cream, vivid orange, chunky rounded type. Sticker accents and hard drop-shadows give Kaki the feel of a friend-group app — closer to BeReal or Duolingo than a fintech tool.

Direction B

Playful Buddy

Built around personality. Rounded sans with extra weight, "scribble" yellow highlight under key words, and hard offset shadows that feel handmade rather than digital. Every step gets a small color shift and a chunky icon. Best for an app that wants to feel like fun with friends — even when the moderation engine is doing serious work under the hood.
